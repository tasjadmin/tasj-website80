# Mobile UX Quick Reference

## 🎯 What Changed?

### Navigation
- ✅ **Menu auto-closes** when you tap a link
- ✅ **Body scroll locks** when menu is open (no background scroll)
- ✅ **Larger tap targets** - 56px tall menu items (easier to tap)
- ✅ **Better spacing** - No accidental taps

### Back to Top Button
- ✅ **Bigger on mobile** - 48px × 48px (was 40px)
- ✅ **Better positioned** - 80px from bottom (doesn't overlap footer)
- ✅ **Smoother taps** - No highlight flash

### Buttons & Links
- ✅ **All buttons 48px+ tall** - Easy to tap with thumb
- ✅ **Full-width on mobile** - No missed taps
- ✅ **Better padding** - More comfortable interaction

### Typography
- ✅ **Optimized font sizes** - Better readability
- ✅ **Better line heights** - Easier to read
- ✅ **No zoom issues** - Consistent text size

### Footer
- ✅ **Larger social icons** - 48px (easier to tap)
- ✅ **Touch-friendly links** - 44px min height
- ✅ **Better forms** - Larger inputs and buttons

### Forms
- ✅ **Larger input fields** - 48px min height
- ✅ **Full-width buttons** - Easy to find and tap
- ✅ **Better spacing** - Comfortable to fill out
